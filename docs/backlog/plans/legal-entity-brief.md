# Legal entity + payments jurisdiction brief

> **This is not legal or tax advice.** Nothing in this document should be treated as a recommendation. It exists to make the conversation with an accountant/immigration-and-tax lawyer precise and short. Every factual claim below is cited to a public source; where public sources disagree or are silent, that is stated explicitly rather than resolved by guessing. Verify every load-bearing fact with counsel before acting on it — tax and platform-eligibility rules for Mexico changed materially in 2025–2026 and may change again before Phase 2 ships.

## Goal

After this lands, Felix can go into a counsel conversation with the exact decision framed, the downstream constraints named, and a numbered question list — instead of "figure out our legal entity" as an open-ended ask.

## Why now

MRT-15-P0-04 found that "legal entity + payments" is filed as an open decision (`idea-v3.md` §15.8) while Phase 2 already commits to specific checkout behavior — deposit/balance capture, OXXO, multi-currency, invoicing — that the entity choice constrains. MRT-15-P1-05 found the plan conflates two distinct obligations (FMM, a federal immigration process, vs. Quintana Roo lodging-reporting, a state obligation) under one vendor claim. Both are cheaper to resolve before Phase 2's payment/booking schema freezes than after.

## 2. Realistic options (tradeoffs only — no recommendation)

| Option                                                                                                                                                                                                   | What it enables                                                                                                                                                                                           | What it forecloses / costs                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mexican entity (RFC-holding persona moral or física con actividad empresarial), Maki as merchant of record**                                                                                           | Full OXXO access, MX installments, native MercadoPago seller account, CFDI issued directly by Maki, MXN settlement (avoids FX drag on Saneamiento/ISH remittance, which are MXN-denominated obligations). | Requires ongoing Mexican corporate/tax compliance (CFDI discipline, monthly ISH remittance, SAT filings) regardless of where Felix is resident; a non-Mexican legal representative can still act but the entity itself must be domiciled in Mexico per Stripe's account requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Foreign entity (e.g., EU/US) as merchant of record, no Mexican payment path**                                                                                                                          | Simpler for a foreign-resident owner to run day-to-day; avoids Mexican corporate tax filings tied to the merchant entity itself.                                                                          | Cannot offer OXXO or MX installments (both require account country = Mexico per Stripe docs above); cannot open a native MercadoPago MX seller account without a Mexican RFC. Guests without international cards (a meaningful share of the MXN-price, cash-preferring segment `idea-v3.md` names) lose a payment option outright. CFDI/ISH/Saneamiento obligations likely still attach to whoever operates the property in Quintana Roo regardless of where the payment entity sits — **this needs explicit counsel confirmation**, since operating a Tulum property is plausibly enough nexus to trigger Mexican tax obligations independent of the payment processor's entity. |
| **Hybrid — foreign entity for general operations, separate Mexican RFC/entity solely for the Mexico-domiciled payment rail (Stripe MX + MercadoPago) and local tax remittance (CFDI, ISH, Saneamiento)** | Gets OXXO/installments/MercadoPago access and local tax compliance without moving the whole business under Mexican corporate law.                                                                         | Two sets of books, two merchant-of-record questions to resolve (which entity actually sells the stay to the guest?), and a real risk of an inconsistent CFDI trail if the invoicing entity and the payment-collecting entity don't match — the exact failure mode a 2026 SAT crackdown on "real, existing, justifiable" transactions would flag. Requires the two entities to have a clean, documented commercial relationship (e.g., a services or agency agreement) that counsel should draft, not assume.                                                                                                                                                                      |

## 3. Precise questions for counsel

Numbered for Felix to send verbatim.

1. Given that Maki operates a physical short-term-rental property in Aldea Zama, Tulum, does Mexican tax nexus (RFC registration, CFDI issuance, ISH/Saneamiento remittance obligations) attach to the property operation itself, independent of which entity is the payment merchant of record? In other words: can a foreign entity be merchant of record while a separate Mexican party (or the same foreign entity registered as a non-resident taxpayer) handles local tax obligations, or does operating the property require a Mexican RFC regardless?
2. If a Mexican entity is formed, does it need to be a persona moral (corporation/LLC), or is a persona física con actividad empresarial sufficient for our transaction volume and for meeting Stripe's and MercadoPago's account-holder requirements cited in §1?
3. Who is the "prestador del servicio" for Quintana Roo ISH purposes if bookings are taken directly on our site rather than through a digital platform like Airbnb — does the 5% (direct) or 6% (platform) rate apply to our direct-booking channel?
4. Please confirm current authority (state vs. municipal) and remittance process for the Derecho de Saneamiento Ambiental as applied in Tulum specifically — our internal documentation currently says "state levy" but the source found in this research describes it as governed by municipal Finance Law; which is correct, and to which authority and by what mechanism/deadline is it remitted?
5. Does the 30% deposit / 70% balance payment structure we're planning (see `idea-v3.md` §9) have any Mexican consumer-protection (PROFECO) implications for cancellation/refund terms that would need to be disclosed differently than a US/EU consumer-facing site?
6. What are our IVA (VAT) obligations on the lodging fee, the cleaning fee, and the Saneamiento pass-through specifically — is Saneamiento itself subject to IVA, or collected outside the IVA base?
7. If we operate a hybrid structure (foreign entity + Mexican payment/tax entity), what commercial-agreement structure between the two entities keeps our CFDI trail defensible under SAT's 2026 "real, existing, justifiable operation" documentation standard (contracts, POs, proof of payment, proof of delivery retained 5+ years)?
8. Are there Quintana Roo Registro Estatal de Turismo (Retur-Q) obligations that attach to the _property_ or the _operating entity_, and do they change based on which entity structure (Mexican vs. foreign vs. hybrid) we choose? (See §4 below — do not assume Retur-Q and FMM are the same thing when answering.)
9. Given we'll take EUR- and USD-priced direct bookings from non-Mexican guests as well as MXN-priced bookings, what account/entity structure minimizes double taxation or double invoicing risk across those currency/guest-residency combinations?

## 4. FMM vs. Quintana Roo lodging-reporting — two distinct obligations

`CONTEXT.md`'s FMM glossary entry already flags these as distinct; this section documents what's publicly verifiable about each, because the vendor claim in `idea-v3.md` §9 ("Chekin-class FMM guest registration handled silently") does not establish coverage of the second one.

|                                 | FMM (federal)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Quintana Roo lodging-reporting (state)                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **What it is**                  | _Forma Migratoria Múltiple_ — a Mexican immigration entry form.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Registro Estatal de Turismo (Retur-Q), administered by Quintana Roo's state tourism authority (Sedetur), a separate state registry for tourism service providers including short-term rental hosts/platforms.                                        |
| **Who it obligates**            | The traveler, at the point of entry — and per INM's own published guidance, air travelers arriving through international airports do not fill out or carry an FMM themselves; immigration authorities generate it automatically at entry. It is only the traveler's own responsibility to complete for land-border crossings. | The lodging **host/operator** (and, per recent enforcement moves, the booking platforms themselves — Airbnb, Booking.com, Home to Go are named as being pushed toward requiring Retur-Q registration from hosts).                                    |
| **What triggers it**            | Immigration entry at the border/airport — has nothing to do with where the traveler stays.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Operating short-term-rental lodging in Quintana Roo, independent of how the guest entered the country.                                                                                                                                               |
| **Enforcement / stakes**        | Not a host compliance item at all for air arrivals per INM's own text.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Only Retur-Q-registered properties are expected to be permitted to operate/list starting 2026; penalties reported up to 100,000 MXN for non-compliance.                                                                                              |
| **Publication date / currency** | INM guidance page, undated on the page itself — treat as current but re-verify at contract time since immigration rules can change.                                                                                                                                                                                           | News coverage dated November 23, 2025, describing 2026 enforcement; ~3,000 providers registered as of that reporting, hotel sector leading compliance.                                                                                               |
| **Sources**                     | https://www.inm.gob.mx/gobmx/word/index.php/paises-no-requieren-visa-para-mexico/, https://www.inm.gob.mx/fmme/publico/solicitud.html                                                                                                                                                                                         | https://quintanaroohoy.com/quintanaroo/solo-alojamientos-con-registro-retur-q-podran-operar-en-2026-advierte-sedetur/, https://community.withairbnb.com/t5/Gestiona-tu-negocio/Requisitos-para-la-COFE-REC-RETURQ-SITURQ-en-Quintana-Roo/m-p/2208827 |

**On the Chekin claim specifically:** searches for Chekin's own Mexico-specific FMM integration documentation returned only Chekin's Spain-focused guest-registration content (Real Decreto 933/2021, the Spanish traveler-registration mandate) — **no Mexico-specific Chekin documentation was found establishing FMM handling, let alone Retur-Q handling.** This is consistent with MRT-15-P1-05's finding: the claim "Chekin handles FMM registration" is unvalidated for Mexico in general, and Retur-Q is not addressed by it at all. Do not treat a Chekin contract as satisfying the Quintana Roo state obligation without a vendor-specific written confirmation naming Retur-Q explicitly.
