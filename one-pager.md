# Maki Tulum — One-Pager

## What It Is

Maki Tulum is a premium direct-booking brand for a small design-led compound in Aldea Zama. The business uses Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO, and other channels to acquire first-time guests, then converts them into repeat guests who book directly on `makitulum.com`.

The goal is not to eliminate OTAs immediately. The goal is to use them for discovery and demand capture, while making direct the preferred path for the second stay and beyond.

## The Core Idea

Most independent properties treat OTAs as both acquisition and retention. That is the mistake.

Maki treats OTAs as **customer acquisition channels** and the direct site as the **relationship and repeat-booking channel**.

The model is simple:

1. A guest discovers Maki on Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO, Instagram, or Google.
2. They book where trust is highest for the first trip.
3. Maki delivers a better-hosted stay than the platform experience alone can offer.
4. The guest leaves with a direct relationship to the property.
5. The next booking happens on `makitulum.com`.

## Why This Can Work

- Repeat direct bookings are structurally more profitable than OTA bookings.
- Tulum is a high-emotion destination where trust, taste, and planning help influence conversion.
- A small compound can feel more personal than a hotel and more credible than a generic short-term rental.
- Maki has a differentiated angle through multilingual hosting, especially German-speaking guests.
- The best guests to win are not anonymous one-time visitors, but returning guests who already trust the property.

## What Makes Maki Different

Maki should not compete by being cheaper or louder. It should compete by being more trusted, more personal, and more specific.

Its edge is the combination of:

- a visually distinctive property
- excellent photo/video assets
- a strong owner voice
- multilingual hosting
- fast WhatsApp communication
- named local partners and honest recommendations
- a smoother pre-arrival and in-stay experience
- repeat-guest recognition and rebooking ease

The moat is not software. The moat is taste, trust, operations, and memory.

## The Product Strategy

### Build

- a premium marketing and booking site
- strong editorial pages for the compound and each unit
- all-in pricing transparency
- direct booking flow
- review aggregation and trust signals
- WhatsApp-first guest communication
- repeat-guest capture and rebooking journeys
- a small but high-quality content/guide layer

### Buy

- PMS / channel manager
- payments
- guest verification
- smart lock integrations
- housekeeping and maintenance tooling
- guest app / pre-arrival tooling if needed

Maki should build only what creates a differentiated guest relationship and direct-booking advantage.

## What Success Requires

This works only if five things are true:

- the property is genuinely desirable
- operations are excellent and consistent
- media assets are strong enough to sell confidence and atmosphere
- guest follow-up is disciplined after checkout
- the team stays focused and does not overbuild software

If the stay is merely good, OTAs will keep winning. Direct rebooking only happens when the guest feels attached to the place and the people behind it.

## What To Measure

Primary strategic metric:

**Percent of eligible repeat guests who rebook direct within 24 months**

Supporting metrics:

- direct share of total bookings
- repeat rate
- repeat-direct rate
- occupancy and ADR by channel
- WhatsApp first-response time
- bundle attach rate
- net revenue by channel

## Why Now

The opportunity is to build a modern independent hospitality brand that does not reject marketplaces, but uses them intelligently. OTAs can fill the top of the funnel. The real value is created when Maki turns that rented demand into an owned guest relationship.

That is how `makitulum.com` becomes more than a booking site. It becomes the place where the second stay starts.
